# Business Scaling Model: The Economics of Generative Infrastructure

## 1. Unit Economics: The "Hybrid" Advantage
Because the Eideus Architecture utilizes a **Federated Cognitive Model** (Local Memory + Cloud Brain), the marginal cost per user is significantly optimized compared to full-cloud competitors.

**Cost Comparison:**
*   **Competitor (Full Cloud):** $0.002 per interaction × 500 interactions = **$1.00/user/month**
*   **Eideus Dawn (Hybrid):** 80% Local Resolution / 20% Cloud Inference = **$0.20/user/month**

**Result:** A **5x Profitability Efficiency** achieved through the "Memory Core" architecture.

## 2. Infrastructure Scaling Paths
The application demonstrates how a consistent $83k/month ($1M/year) infrastructure spend supports two distinct revenue models.

### Path A: B2C Gaming (Eideus Dawn)
*   **Operating Cost:** ~$83,000/month (Server Bill)
*   **User Base:** ~415,000 Active Users.
*   **Projected Revenue:** At a 5% monthly monetization rate (Industry Standard for Deep Tech/Sim), this infrastructure supports **$2M/month** in gross revenue.
*   **Scalability:** Horizontal scaling via standard container orchestration.

### Path B: B2B Enterprise (City SeCUReD)
*   **Operating Cost:** ~$10,000/month per City Instance (Includes dedicated compute).
*   **Client Base:** 8 Municipal Contracts.
*   **Projected Revenue:** Enterprise contracts typically range from **$500k - $1M per year** per deployment.
*   **Scalability:** Vertical scaling per dedicated instance.

The Google for Startups Cloud Program credits ($350,000) provide approximately **4 years of operational runway** at initial scale, allowing the company to reach critical mass (Path A) or secure initial contracts (Path B) without dilutive capital requirements.
